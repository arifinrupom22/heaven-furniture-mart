About Heaven Furniture Mart

Where Craftsmanship
Meets Your Vision

Founded in 2020 by Abul Kalam Bhuiyan, Heaven Furniture Mart was born from a simple belief: furniture should be built around people, not the other way around.

From our showroom on Agrabad Access Road in Chattogram, we design and craft bespoke furniture that reflects your space, your taste, and your lifestyle. Every piece is made with precision, using premium wood and materials that stand the test of time.

We don't sell mass-produced furniture. We create pieces that belong uniquely to you.
